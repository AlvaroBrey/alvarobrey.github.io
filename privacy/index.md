# Privacy

alvarobrey.com is a personal site. It has no accounts, no forms, no advertising and no tracking cookies. This page describes the little data that is collected when you visit.

## What your browser stores

One value, under the key `alvarobrey.com.darkTheme`, remembering whether you picked the light or the dark theme. It stays in your browser, is never sent anywhere, and clearing your site data removes it.

## Analytics

The site loads [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/), which counts page views without cookies and without fingerprinting visitors. It records the page URL, the referrer, and coarse details such as browser and country. It does not build a profile of you, and I cannot identify you from it.

## Hosting

The site is a set of static files served by [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) through [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/). Both keep short-lived server logs, including IP addresses, for operations and abuse prevention, under their own privacy policies.

## Links away from here

Pages here link out to GitHub, LinkedIn and elsewhere. Once you follow one of those links, that site's own privacy policy applies instead of this one.

## Questions

Nothing collected here is sold or shared with anyone. If you have a question about any of it, [get in touch](https://alvarobrey.com/contact).
